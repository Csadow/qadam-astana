# Qadam — short usability session

Status: **protocol prepared; no participant sessions recorded yet**. Run with 3–5 volunteers before the pitch if time permits. This is a small qualitative check, not proof of demand or statistical accuracy.

Allow about eight minutes per person. Use the local app or an authorized hosted session. Ask permission to note observations; no name, precise location, account details or recording is needed. Let participants stop at any time. Use P1–P5 as anonymous labels and an app preset as the origin. Do not silently coach them through the controls.

## Tasks

1. “You are near EXPO with an hour free. You want a park and do not want coffee. Find a plan you would consider following.” Observe whether the participant notices the interpretation, estimated return and Demo time. Record time to a decision, errors and help needed.
2. Reset to the EXPO demo. “Keep the park, try a different café, then undo the change.” Observe whether the participant finds the controls and understands the changed duration. Ask them to remove a stop and explain the result.
3. Show the quiet-coffee scenario. “What does this screen actually confirm about sockets, quietness and walking access?” Record misunderstandings, particularly if unknown information is read as a promise.

Use mobile for at least two participants. After the tasks, ask: “Would you follow this plan? Why?”, “What was unclear?”, and “How easy was it to decide, from 1 to 5?” Preserve criticisms in the notes; do not turn a polite reaction into a recommendation.

## Reporting in the pitch

Only after sessions happen, report the actual count, task and observations. Example format: “We observed N people on these tasks; X completed without help; these two problems remain.” Do not invent values or replace the engineering checks with a satisfaction claim. Until then, the pitch correctly says that participant testing is pending.
